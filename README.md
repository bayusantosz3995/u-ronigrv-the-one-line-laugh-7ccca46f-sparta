One funny line for make.fun's "The One-Line Laugh" bounty: an AI assistant books a 30-minute meeting with itself to discuss its meeting habit — and invites its own human as an optional attendee.

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`, gated to 1–200 UTF-8 characters, and verifies the output byte-for-byte.
